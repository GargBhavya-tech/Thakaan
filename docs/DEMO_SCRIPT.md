# Demo script (about 4 minutes)

Everything on this path works with the phone in airplane mode. Say "replay" out loud whenever the chip says REPLAY.

1. **Set the scene (20 s).** A delivery rider, 41 C, noon. Open Home. Provenance chip shows REAL for live sensors.
2. **Replay the shift (60 s).** Tap Replay at 20x. The gauge goes Cool, Warming, Strained. The forecast line says when Strained is expected. The chip says REPLAY.
3. **The alert (30 s).** At Strained the phone speaks one action in the worker's language with its time cost, and vibrates. Switch language in setup to show Hindi, then Kannada.
4. **Chhaya Route (60 s).** Open Chhaya. At Cool the route is the fastest. At Strained the same trip detours to shade and adds a water stop, with the extra seconds shown. At Danger it points to the nearest cool seated stop.
5. **Quick check (60 s).** A teammate who has never seen it does the Quick check for their kind of work (Settings > Your work; about 80 s for the general check, about 2 minutes for a delivery partner, whose check includes the face check).
6. **Supervisor console (30 s).** Tap Copy status, paste into the console on the laptop. Show the level and coarse reasons, and that no raw numbers are visible. Show the exposure log verifier going green, change one byte, show it go red.
7. **Fail-safe (20 s).** Safety tab: 6/6 PASS, flip one guard OFF, its row turns red.
8. **Close with the limits (20 s).** Read the first three bullets of `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.

Backup if the camera misbehaves: skip step 5 and show the Safety tab instead. Backup if the phone is unavailable: run the Office Kit mirror.
