Thakaanथकान

Thakaan means exhaustion. The people most exposed to heat are the riders, builders and street workers who carry a phone all day but no wearable. Thakaan turns that phone into a personal heat-strain guardian that senses the body, the brain and the sun, and steers the worker to shade and water before heat takes them down. Everything runs on the phone, offline.

Body · strain from the heartbeat Brain · tremor, taps, gait, face Sun · light, WBGT, shade routing

01Who it serves

One person is protected on the phone. A supervisor sees only levels. Nothing about a worker's body leaves the phone unless the worker shares it.

Primary userDelivery rider

Scooter or motorcycle, paid per task, phone on the handlebar. Needs a warning at a stop, a shaded route, a water stop on the way, and every instruction in their own language with its time cost.

Primary userConstruction worker

Phone in the pocket, put down at breaks. Needs break-time check-ins, rest pacing and a collapse alarm that a bystander can act on.

Primary userStreet worker

Traffic police, vendors, farm workers. Needs exposure and rest prompts and the same collapse alarm.

OperatorSite supervisor or fleet lead

Laptop console. Sees each worker's level, trend, minutes since rest and coarse reasons. Never raw heart rate, scores or voice, so the data cannot be used to rate or deactivate anyone.

03The phone (everything runs here)

There is no server in the core loop. The safety-critical path runs on the phone, so a warning still fires in a dead zone, in a basement or on a crowded road. The laptop console is an optional viewer.

Inputs

  • Heart rate by fingertip on the rear camera and torch (primary), optionally from the face through the front camera. No wearable.
  • Accelerometer and gyro continuously; ambient light for sun or shade; GNSS for position, speed and sky view.
  • Touch timing for the tapping task; tremor from the accelerometer while the phone is held out.
  • A daily 3-tap check-in: slept well, ate, feel unwell. Profile entered once at onboarding.

On the phone

  • ECTemp Kalman filter with a heat-balance prior, one step per minute, with variance that grows across gaps.
  • Liljegren WBGT through a native library (lwbgt over JNI).
  • Brain battery: robust z-scores against the same worker, same phone, same placement and time of day, with CUSUM change detection.
  • Offline routing on a bundled OpenStreetMap graph with a precomputed shade layer.
  • Spoken alerts through the phone's own text-to-speech voices.

Keeps working when

  • Airplane mode is on: routing, sensing, scoring and alerts need no network.
  • A sensor is missing or stale: the level holds or rises, it never drops.
  • The model is unavailable: fixed template lines replace the language model.
  • OriginOS tries to kill background work: a foreground service owns the sensors.
"शरीर गरम हो रहा है। 3 मिनट आगे छाया और पानी है।"
"Your body is heating up. There is shade and water 3 minutes ahead." Every alert names one action, speaks it in the worker's language (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu or English) and shows its time cost.

05Engines

The science lives here as plain, testable Kotlin. A language model never computes a level, a pulse or a route. Every engine is unit-tested on a laptop, and the ones that need the phone are checked on the iQOO 15 (see chapter 11 for which).

Body · heat strain from the heartbeat

Body

Core-temperature trend (ECTemp)

State: CTₜ = CTₜ₋₁ + w, w ~ N(0, Q), Q = 0.000484 Observe: HRₜ = a·CTₜ² + b·CTₜ + c + v, v ~ N(0, R) a = −4.5714 b = 384.4286 c = −7887.1 R = 356.4544 (18.88 bpm) NIS gate = 3.84 Start: CT₀ = 37.1 °C, v₀ = 0.02 (0.2 if already hot) Step: once a minute; between readings predict only

Reproduces all seven published test cases with personalisation off. Thakaan gets heart rate at stops, not every minute, so the variance grows (never shrinks) across a gap. The estimate is never shown as a number.

Heat-balance prior and HR rule

  • A heat-balance prior (metabolic load, solar load, clothing, wind) keeps the estimate running when heart rate is missing, and the uncertainty band widens.
  • Plain ECTemp stays runnable behind a switch.
  • An independent heart-rate rule backs it up: sustained resting HR above 90 to 100 bpm raises the level.
  • Hydration deficit comes from modelled sweat minus logged drinks.

Output

  • Core trend with its band, used only as evidence for the level.
  • Anchors fire at 38.5 °C or less (38.0 °C before acclimatisation); no action waits for an estimate above 39 °C, where ECTemp is blind.
  • Excess HR, idle-state drift, hydration deficit, acclimatisation (starts at 0 days, the cautious default).

Pulse measurement

Body

Fingertip (primary)

  • Rear camera with torch, manual exposure aimed during a 10 s settle then frozen, 30 fps, red, green and blue means of a centre patch.
  • Two ways to accept a capture: the strict red-only beat test (perfusion, template correlation, signal-to-noise, dropped frames), or the colour-combining test below.
  • Thermal guard blocks the check on a hot phone; torch dimming is detected.

Colour-combining (POS) test

1. POS over R, G, B cancels changes common to all colours 2. read the rate in 8 s windows, step 2 s 3. accept only if ≥ 75% of windows are within 3 bpm of the median AND one independent colour view (G−B or G−R) agrees within 5 bpm

Built from four real captures: a still phone on a table gave 61 to 63 bpm in every window, while hand-held captures wandered between 52 and 121 bpm and are rejected. The true rate is not yet confirmed against a counted pulse.

Face (optional, never replaces the finger)

  • Front camera and MediaPipe landmarks; POS on forehead and cheek colour; breathing from a 30 s or longer window.
  • Rejects a dark or bright scene, a moving head, a lost face, dropped frames, and any pulse that is not steady.
  • Shown for information only; it never reaches the risk engine.

Brain battery

Brain

Tests

  • Hand hold: 4 to 12 Hz tremor power from the accelerometer, flagged if taken just after lifting.
  • Tapping: Fitts-style alternation between two targets; timestamps come from the touch event, never a UI callback.
  • Gait: stride variability and sway while walking, per placement.
  • Face fatigue: blink rate, eyelid-closure share (PERCLOS at 80%), long closures, sustained yawns, gaze drift.
  • Speech code and a short reaction test are specified; the recogniser is not built yet.

Change detection

z = (x − median) / (1.4826 · MAD) own baseline CUSUM on z, capped steps, alarm after a run of mildly worse results (one bad test never alarms) Families: MOTION · TOUCH · VOICE · FACE · NAVIGATION Two families flagged → at least Strained

Scores are only compared with the same worker, on the same phone, same placement and time-of-day bucket. No baseline means no judgement, never a calming signal.

Context

  • The check-in can only lower the weight of a brain flag when exposure is low and a benign cause is known.
  • Unknown answers are neutral, never benign.
  • Squinting in bright light is rejected for the face test instead of being read as tiredness.

Exposure and sun

Sun

Sun, shade, overcast

  • Ambient-light classifier with tilt correction and a body-shadow guard (a low sun behind the rider is hidden from a phone facing the rider).
  • Moving into sun is accepted after 2 s, out of sun only after 5 s.
  • Unknown counts as sun. A blocked sensor or lost GNSS moves the answer toward sun, never away.

Heat index

WBGT Liljegren model (lwbgt, native, via JNI) UTCI secondary, not used for anchors Never: the phone body as a globe thermometer, the ACSM87 formula by day

Missing weather or a failed native call returns "unknown", which the risk engine treats as hot.

Ledgers

  • Sun dose in minutes, rest ledger (time since a real rest: shade plus still), hydration log.
  • Stop detector and activity state gate when a heart-rate reading may be compared with another.

Risk engine · Thakaan Strain Score

Shared

Three layers

  • Exposure prior from sun, WBGT, rest and time of day. Always computed, so the score never goes blank.
  • Body update from the core trend and excess HR, weighted by how fresh and clean the last reading was.
  • Brain override from the brain battery and symptoms.

Levels

TSS 0..100 = 100 · σ( wE·E + wB·B·q + wN·N + b ) Level = the highest any anchor or the score supports Cool · Warming · Strained · Danger Event = Collapse (fall, then no movement)

Hysteresis and minimum dwell stop flapping; Danger does not clear without a passed recovery check.

Fail-safe guards

  • Six switchable guards, each with a scenario that must turn red when the guard is off: hold stale body evidence, exposure floor on stale HR, unknown shade is sun, missing weather is hot, two brain families force Strained, the language model cannot change a level.
  • A property test compares a degraded run with a reference run and fails if any degradation lowers the level.

Chhaya Route · strain-aware shade routing

Sun

Cost function

cost(edge) = time × (1 + λ(TSS) · sun_fraction) + heat-island penalty on sunny arterials λ = 0 below TSS 30 → the route is the fastest route Detour budget: Warming +1 min · Strained +3 min Danger: drop the destination, go to the nearest cool seat

At Strained the router inserts the best listed water stop whose detour fits the budget.

Shade layer

  • Sun position ray-cast against OpenStreetMap buildings and trees, precomputed per road segment and time slot.
  • Computed, not measured. About 95% of building heights are guessed, so low-confidence segments are treated as sunnier. Every route shows the share that rests on guesses.
  • Rider light readings are specified to correct the layer; that feedback loop is not built.

Output

  • Route with "+N min, X% shade" and the water stop, drawn on a bundled map with the OpenStreetMap credit.
  • Water points are shown as "listed, quality unverified".
  • Works with no network.

Motion and collapse

Brain

Detect

  • Activity (riding, walking, still), stop detector, posture clock.
  • Collapse: free fall plus impact, a bike tip-over, or a slump (a large orientation change) followed by about eight seconds of stillness.

Never a siren first

  • The event only starts a 15 s countdown ("Are you OK?"). A spoken "haan" or an "I'm OK" tap cancels it, and clears the alarm if it already started.
  • Only after the countdown does the alarm and a bystander message with an SMS preview appear. Nothing is sent without a tap.

06Actions and guards

The risk engine decides the level; everything after that only says it and shows it. Every alert names one action, speaks it in the worker's language and shows what it costs in time.

LevelHapticActionScreen and extras
WarmingTwo soft pulsesDrink water at the next stop (1 min)Amber ring; small detours allowed
StrainedThree strong pulses, repeatedShade and water ahead (3 min)Orange banner with the route; Chhaya Route switches on; tap check at the next stop
DangerContinuous until acknowledgedStop now, sit in shade (2 min)Red takeover with the nearest shade; contact and supervisor alerted; cannot be snoozed
CollapseSiren and flash after the countdownBystander message: call 108First-aid steps, SMS preview with location

Guards in front of every message

  • Alerts fire on a rise only, through one plan builder, so a level held for an hour does not repeat itself.
  • Explainer validator. The template line is always built first. A model line is accepted only if it states the engine's minute figure, adds no other number, mentions no temperature or pulse, offers no reassurance, and names no stronger or gentler action than the engine chose. Otherwise the template is used. The level and the action never come from model text.
  • Safety checks tab. The same six guards, each with a hidden switch that turns its check red, because a check that cannot fail proves nothing.
  • Provenance. Every reading is stamped REAL, REPLAY or SIMULATED at the sensor hub and everything computed from it inherits the stamp. A replay can never show as REAL.
  • Exposure log. A hash chain signed with a hardware-backed key. It proves entries were not edited after recording, not that the observation was true.

09Trust, safety and privacy

A heat tool can easily become a tool to rate workers. The design prevents that in code, and states its limits plainly.

The worker owns the data

  • Raw physiology (heart rate, HRV, core trend, brain scores, symptoms) never leaves the phone.
  • Camera frames and audio live in memory only and are dropped after each check.
  • The app declares no network permission, which is checked on the installed build. Nothing can be sent.
  • The console sees levels, a trend, minutes since rest and coarse reasons. Position is shared only if the worker turns it on.

Never lower, never hide

  • Losing or degrading information can never lower a level; the safety property test and the on-screen switches prove it.
  • The strain levels are Cool, Warming, Strained and Danger, never a body-temperature number.
  • Provenance chips on every reading; replay is never passed off as live.

Honest limits

  • A heat-strain wellbeing tool, not a medical device. It advises rest; it does not diagnose.
  • ECTemp is covered by US patent 10,702,165, so commercial use needs a licence.
  • Shade is computed from map data, not measured. The hash chain proves editing, not truth.
  • Face and forehead checks are optional, experimental and unvalidated against a reference.

13Judging fit

CriterionWhat Thakaan shows
Problem and impactHeat illness attacks judgement before the body gives up, and the people most exposed are paid per task and cannot afford a wearable. They already carry the sensor.
Uses the phone's hardwareCamera and torch for the pulse, the accelerometer for tremor and collapse, the touchscreen for the brain test, the light sensor and GNSS for the sun, an on-device model runtime, Keystore for the proof log. No cloud, no wearable.
Technical depthA published core-temperature filter reproduced exactly, a Liljegren WBGT in native code, change detection against each worker's own baseline, strain-aware offline routing, and a pulse method that was tuned on real captures.
Honesty and safetyProvenance on every reading, six guard switches that turn red, a property test that no degradation lowers a level, and this page's ledger of what is and is not verified.
Design for the userCool, Warming, Strained, Danger by colour, shape and word, spoken in five languages, with the time cost shown, and never blocking the job.

14Demo script

About four minutes. Everything on this path works with the phone in airplane mode. Say "replay" out loud whenever the chip says REPLAY.

0:00–0:20

A delivery rider at noon in 41 °C. Open Home: the chip says REAL for the live sensors.

0:20–1:20

Replay the shift at 20x. The gauge goes Cool, Warming, Strained and the forecast line says when Strained is expected. The chip says REPLAY.

1:20–1:50

At Strained the phone speaks one action in the worker's language with its time cost, and vibrates. Switch the language from Hindi to Kannada.

1:50–2:50

Chhaya Route. At Cool the route is the fastest. At Strained the same trip detours to shade and adds a water stop, with the extra minutes shown. At Danger it points to the nearest cool seat.

2:50–3:50

A teammate who has never seen it does the Quick check: finger on the camera, hold still, tap, how do you feel.

3:50–4:20

Paste the phone's sealed status into the console: level and coarse reasons, no raw numbers. Change one byte of the exposure log and watch the verifier turn red.

4:20–4:40

Safety checks tab: all PASS, flip one guard off, its row turns red. Close with the limits from the ledger.

Backups: if the camera misbehaves, skip the Quick check and show the Safety tab; if the phone is unavailable, run the Office Kit mirror.
