# Thakaan: what it does not do (read before you trust it)

Thakaan is a heat-strain wellbeing tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose heat illness. ECTemp is patent-covered (US 10,702,165), so commercial use needs a licence.

## What is estimated, not measured
- **Shade is computed, not measured.** It is ray-cast from OpenStreetMap buildings. About 95% of building heights are guessed (3 storeys), so roughly half the street segments are marked low-confidence and treated as more sunny. Trees and awnings are not modelled. Measured corrections from riders only move the estimate slowly.
- **Core temperature is an estimate.** It is a filtered inference from heart rate (and a heat-balance prior), never shown as a number. The worker sees Cool, Warming, Strained or Danger.
- **Fingertip pulse** needs a still finger and a cool phone. Bad captures are rejected, not guessed.
- **WBGT** comes from a cached forecast plus the phone's light sensor, not a globe thermometer. The weather cache covers only 8 to 13 October 2026.

## What is not built or not proven yet
- Verified on the iQOO 15: Keystore signing and tamper detection, ECTemp 7/7, the native WBGT library, offline routing, replay labelling, the Safety checks, five installed voices. **Not yet verified on the phone:** a fingertip pulse that passes the quality gates (none has yet), how the spoken alerts sound, GNSS outdoors and the light sensor in sun and shade. See `docs/DEVICE_RESULTS.md`.
- The language model (Gemma) is wired in and loads on the phone's GPU. It can only re-word a line the risk engine already chose, a wording check rejects any line that changes the numbers, and the template line is the fallback.
- The speech-code test uses the Vosk digit grammar, which has not been tried with a real voice. The real recorded ride is not captured yet; the demo uses replay and says so (the REPLAY chip).
- H3 cells were skipped; street segments act as cells.

## What the audit log proves
The hash chain and signature prove an entry was not edited after it was recorded. They do not prove the observation was true.

## Privacy limits
Pulse, brain scores and symptoms stay on the phone. The supervisor console sees the level, a trend, minutes since rest and coarse reasons. Position is shared only if the worker turns it on. A person who holds the unlocked phone can see everything on it.

## Safety behaviour you can rely on
Losing or degrading information never lowers the level: missing heart rate, lost location, a blocked light sensor or an unavailable model hold or raise it. This is checked by a property test and by the Safety checks tab.

## Added 2026-10-10 (models, air dose, floods, chest)
- Air dose uses a modelled PM2.5 value for the area (Open-Meteo / Copernicus CAMS), not a station reading and not the worker's own air; no OpenAQ key was obtained. Ventilation from heart rate uses the Greenwald 2019 pooled model, whose single estimates can be off by about 45% (middle half), averaging out over a shift; between heart-rate checks it is a workload estimate. Typical lung capacity and an unknown sex (middle value) are used until entered. The dose appears on Home; the shift report shows levels only.
- Flood reports: a photo freshness check stops stale or blank reports but cannot prove a road is flooded, so reports show as "reported flooded", never a depth. The crew mesh is built as Bluetooth LE extended advertising only (Wi-Fi Aware is not built) with buddy pairing by a shared code; it has laptop-tested relay rules but has not been run between two phones, and it needs the phone to report extended-advertising support. A report heard from the mesh closes roads like a local one but is anonymous and unverified: anyone nearby with the app could send a false one, so reports are limited to 3 hops, 20 an hour and 3 km, and can only add closures. Rain onset is a cue from light and the cached forecast, not a forecast.
- Chest vibration heart rate works only seated and still and has not been compared with a fingertip yet. Its learned denoiser was trained on simulated data. The continuous version (ScgStream, run by the shift engine) was tuned and tested on synthetic recordings only; the thresholds (motion 0.15 m/s2, tilt 4 deg, 30 s settle, 8% method agreement) are untuned on real people. Alone it can only raise concern (a sustained resting rate above 90 starts the high-rest-HR clock); it never counts as fresh heart evidence and a low chest rate never clears that clock. Breathing rate from the same recording is a by-product, unvalidated.
- The strain GRU, activity CNN, chest denoiser and line-of-sight trees were trained on simulated data (tools/models/train_models.py), run as shadow models and never decide a level; the GRU is no better than the linear forecast and is not used.
- Face pulse, breathing, heart variability and the oxygen estimate come from skin colour and are unconfirmed against a reference; the oxygen figure is uncalibrated unless the worker enters an oximeter reading.
- Whisper-tiny (Speech lab) is advisory and weak in Hindi and weaker in Kannada; the spoken digit and haan/nahi steps stay on the Vosk grammar, and no transcript ever changes a level.
- The shift report is built from the readings the phone had: time with no readings is shown as its own line and never as Cool time; the copy button hands out levels only.
- Chest vibration, first real recordings (2026-10-10, phone lifted onto the chest by hand): the beat finder works on a real chest (ten windows agreed at 80 to 86 beats a minute, near the 83 the face reading gave), but the 30 s posture lockout that follows lifting the phone dropped every still window, so the lab said "Not still enough". The lockout is unchanged until a fingertip or watch comparison exists; the lab now gives 10 s to place the phone, shows what the windows would have said as "not confirmed", and shows which gates cost how many windows. No reference pulse was taken, so the 80 to 86 is not validated.
- Chest vibration, third and fourth real recordings (2026-10-10 evening): a phone held loosely upright gave no steady repeat at all (beat-band vibration 0.07 m/s2); one leaning against the chest gave a steady pulse (about 64 to 71 beats a minute from three independent readings) but only 56% of the 5 s windows fell within 4 beats of the middle one, so the 70% gate rejected it. The 4 beat tolerance was tuned on simulated data and a resting pulse drifts by a few beats in 45 s; it is unchanged until a fingertip or watch reading exists. The lab shows such a reading as "not confirmed" with its spread, and shows contact strength and repeat strength so a loose hold is visible.
- Chest lab tolerance: the lab (display only) now accepts window rates within 6 beats of the middle one instead of 4, from two real recordings against a counted pulse of 70 (medians 70.6 and 73.2; 78% and 89% of windows within 6). That is one person and one counted pulse. The continuous chest stream that feeds the risk engine stays at 4 beats until more people and reference readings exist.
- Two panels, built 2026-10-11, untested on a person or a bike. **Delivery partners** (Ride tab): a pickup check (face, pulse, how you feel) gives one answer, fit to ride, rest 5 minutes and drink water, or look again. Fit needs a face that was read and a level below Strained; an unreadable face is never fit; any symptom or a Strained or Danger level means rest (Danger offers no "ride anyway"). While riding, the front camera watches in windows of 42 s with 80 s off (30 s off after a drowsy window), only above about 11 km/h for 8 s, off when stopped, within 150 m of the drop point, or when the phone is hot; each window reads eyes and yawns only (no pulse). The drowsiness limits (PERCLOS at or above 0.15, two long closures or two yawns in a window) are conservative design values, not tuned on riders, and a helmet visor, sunglasses, glare, night, vibration and a handlebar mount are all untested. The map beside the score is the offline road graph of the demo window only (3.3 km in Bengaluru), directions say what to do and how far because the graph has no street names, and the arrival time uses road-class speeds and the typical traffic of the hour, not live traffic. A simulated ride (SIMULATED) moves along the route without GPS. **Everyone else** (Today tab): a check on a schedule (every 45 minutes at Cool, 30 at Warming, 15 at Strained, 10 at Danger; the reminder vibrates, speaks and posts a notification) and the next 20 minutes: the chance of reaching Strained from the score's rising trend, the heat index expected from the cached forecast, rising fine dust and little wind. That chance is a model with fixed push sizes chosen to lean toward warning (3 points per degree of heat-index rise, 2 for dust, 3 when the weather is unknown); it was not fitted to data, it can only add concern, and it is not a measurement.
- The Quick check differs by kind of work (Settings > Your work): every kind includes the face check (take off any helmet, goggles or cap for it); delivery partners are asked face, pulse and how they feel (about 90 s), construction workers pulse, face, hand hold and symptoms, police or security pulse, face, tapping and symptoms, other gig workers the general check. The general check now takes about two minutes because the face check added 40 s to the original 90 s goal. These choices are the team's assumptions about each job, not yet tested with those workers (ticket #65). Leaving the app mid-check resets the check. What the worker sees: a reading is shown as a plain number only when it passed its quality checks; otherwise no number is shown and the worker is told what to do (hold still, try again). The detail of what passed and failed stays in the Diagnostics labs and in these documents; the data-source limits (shade, traffic, air, the outlook) are in Settings > About.
- Map layers added 2026-10-10 (laptop-tested, not yet seen on the phone): Phone signal, Traffic and Air. Phone signal is this phone's own radio level on streets it has passed (a street needs 3 samples); it differs by network and phone and is not a coverage map. Traffic is an ASSUMED typical pattern by road type and hour, not live traffic and not measured; only streets you rode show a measured speed. Air is one modelled area value (CAMS via Open-Meteo, about 11 km grid) laid over the whole map: it cannot tell one street from another, and its bands are India's 24-hour CPCB scale applied to an hourly value. None of the three changes a route or a level. The modelled flood-prone layer was not built (no elevation or drain data was fetched, by your choice); only rider flood reports exist.
